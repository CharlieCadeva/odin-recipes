McDonalds Cheeseburgers

Steamed burgers deliver an unmistakably juicy texture by cooking entirely through steam rather than direct pan frying.

Melting the cheese directly onto the steamed patties creates a rich and savory bite.

Cooking with steam locks in moisture and yields an exceptionally soft bun and perfectly melted cheddar crust.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound (450g) ground beef (80/20 blend)
  • 4 soft burger buns
  • 4 thick slices sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
  • Dill pickle slices
  • Yellow mustard

Steps:

  1. Divide ground beef into four equal portions and shape into compact round patties.
  2. Season both sides of the patties generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Set up a skillet with a tight-fitting lid or a steam cabinet, adding a splash of water over medium heat.
  4. Place the patties on a perforated tray or directly in the covered pan to steam for 8 to 10 minutes.
  5. Place a slice of sharp cheddar cheese over each patty during the final 2 minutes of steaming until completely melted.
  6. Steam the burger buns cut-side down for 1 minute until warm and pillowy.
  7. Assemble the patties onto the buns, top with pickles and mustard, and serve warm.
