Delicious Home-Made Pizza

This easy pizza dough recipe is great for beginners and produces two soft and chewy homemade pizza crusts. You only need six basic ingredients to begin, skip the delivery and enjoy fresh crust from scratch. Every great pizza begins with a great crust: soft and chewy with a delicious crisp and awesome flavor.

Ingredients:

  • 1 and 1/3 cups (320ml) warm water
  • 1 Tablespoon (13g) granulated sugar
  • 2 and 1/4 teaspoons (7g) instant yeast
  • 3 and 1/2 to 4 cups (438-500g) unbleached all-purpose flour
  • 2 Tablespoons (30ml) extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Sprinkle of cornmeal for dusting the pan

Steps:

  1. Whisk warm water, granulated sugar, and yeast together in a bowl, then let it rest for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the flour, olive oil, and salt, then beat until the dough pulls away from the sides.
  3. Knead the dough for 5 full minutes until it passes the windowpane test and bounces back when poked.
  4. Place dough in a greased bowl, cover tightly, and allow to rise for 60 to 90 minutes until doubled in size.
  5. Preheat the oven to 475°F (246°C) and prepare your baking sheet or pizza pan with olive oil and cornmeal.
  6. Punch down the dough, divide in half, and stretch into a 12-inch circle.
  7. Add olive oil, sauce, mozzarella cheese, and your favorite toppings, then bake for 13 to 15 minutes until golden brown.
